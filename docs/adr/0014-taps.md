# ADR 0014: Taps, the Relic-gated withdrawal that keeps the Streak

Status: accepted for this repository. Not audited, not deployed.

## Context

**What the spec requires** (v5.5 §9 and §11; the config's `relics[].perks.tap`, `heat.tapDrawdownMode`, `copy.tapNotice` and `execution.seals.tapBeforeMaturity`):
- **The tiers.** Blaze (a 90-day Streak) may Tap 10% every 90 days. Eternal (365 days) may Tap 25% every 90 days, and Bedrock (730 days) 25% every 60 days.
- **Youngest lots first, and nothing else changes.** "Taps instead draw youngest lots first with no financial-age haircut or Streak reset. They still pay the normal withdrawal fee and timer and consume a fixed quota" (§9). The config's notice reads: "A Tap keeps your {streak}. The {withdrawFee}% fee and the cooling timer still apply."
- **A Seal is not spared.** "A Tap during an active Seal forfeits its unvested bonus: a Streak exception is not a Seal exception" (§9; `tapBeforeMaturity: forfeitUnvestedBonus`).
- **Perks need the current state.** "Streak is chronological, separate from financial age. Relics remain wallet history; current perks require active state rather than possession of an old NFT" (§9). A Deed sale resets the Streak for the new owner, and wallet Relics do not transfer (§5).

**Where this has to live.** A Tap is an exit, and ADR 0012 keeps every exit in the pool. ForgePool is immutable, so the hook must exist at deployment.

## Decision

**1. `requestTap(id, amount)` is `requestCooling` with three differences.** Both run through one internal `_request(id, amount, tap)`.
- **It is gated and rationed by the Streak.**
  - The position's Streak at request picks the highest tier it has reached.
  - Below 90 days the call reverts `NoTap`.
  - It reverts `TapTooSoon` inside that tier's window since the last Tap, and `TapQuotaExceeded` above the tier's share.
- **It freezes the youngest lots first.**
  - Lots are taken in ascending financial age at request, ties going to the lower slot, and only the last lot drawn is partial.
  - Pro-rata cooling (ADR 0004) is unchanged for ordinary requests.
- **Its ticket carries `tap = true`**, as does its `CoolingRequested` event.

**2. What a Tap shares with cooling.**
- **The plan's timer and allowance (§4).** A Tap counts toward the cumulative request plan and gets that plan's tier timer.
- **The rest of the ticket's life:**
  - the execution window and the lapse;
  - cancel, which resumes the frozen state;
  - the 32-lot capacity;
  - the listing lock (ADR 0011);
  - the Seal pause while it is pending.

**3. Executing a Tap ticket.**
- **No age haircut.** The lots that stay keep their age, and so do other pending slices.
- **No Streak reset.**
- **The normal 1% fee.**
- **It forfeits a running Seal.** It is a Streak exception, not a Seal exception.
- An ordinary execution is unchanged: it haircuts every surviving lot and every other pending slice, a pending Tap's included, and resets the Streak.

**4. Readings marked provisional** ([P]; the spec gives the shares without their base):
- **The share is of active principal at request.** Frozen principal does not count toward it: it is already leaving.
- **The quota is used at request.** `lastTapAt` is set when the Tap is requested. A cancel or a lapse does not give it back, just as a cancel does not give back the plan's allowance (ADR 0004). Without this, request-and-cancel would reset the window at will.
- **Eligibility is checked at request only.** A Tap requested within quota still executes as a Tap after an ordinary execution has reset the Streak in between. That ordinary execution haircuts the Tap's pending slices.
- **The window is the current tier's.** The next Tap waits the interval of the tier the Streak has reached at that request, counted from the last Tap. An Eternal Tap at day 670 therefore allows a Bedrock Tap at day 730. The spec does not say which tier's interval applies after a promotion. `test_tapTierEdges` pins this reading.
- **The tier reads the position's real Streak, and nothing else.** "Streak is chronological, separate from financial age" (§9). Ignition's head start is financial age, a "finite head-start credit" that "cannot be ... sold as financial age through a Deed" (§2; the config's `heatHeadStart: ignitionStart`). First Flame is "the existing 15-day financial-Heat floor" (§2). Neither is Streak.
  - So the head start and the floor never bring a Tap tier closer.
  - The Kindling hook must keep it so. A reading that dates Kindling positions' Streak to Ignition would have to change this deliberately.

**5. Deeds.**
- A Deed's settlement starts a new Streak (ADR 0011) and leaves `lastTapAt` as it was.
- This is safe only while the first tier's Streak (90 days) is at least the longest Tap interval (90 days). By the time a buyer reaches Blaze, the seller's window has closed, so the buyer can never be blocked by the seller's Tap, nor Tap inside the seller's window.
- The generator fails the build if a config breaks that inequality.

**6. Storage.**
- `Ticket.tap` takes the free byte after `sliceMask` (byte 27).
- `Position.lastTapAt` takes word 2, bytes 16–20.
- ForgeView decodes both. `test_tapFieldsDecode` pins the byte offsets against the pool's own getters. `pnpm check:layout` pins only the top-level slots, which are unchanged.

## Capacity

- **The 32-slot limit binds exactly as for cooling.** A lot drawn in part keeps an active remainder and reserves one slot for its slice to return to.
- **Youngest-first splits at most one lot per Tap.**
- **At 32 lots, a Tap that must split a lot reverts `LotCapacity`; a Tap of whole lots succeeds.**
  - The scenario `tapLotCapacity` replays both.
  - The interface should offer the nearest whole-lot amount when a Tap would split at capacity.

## Verification

- **Differential replays:** the BigInt model (`forge-model.ts`) implements the tiers, the window, the quota, youngest-first and the haircut-free execution independently of the contract. Nine new scenarios replay against it, with the full state hash after every step and matching reverts:
  - `tapCycle`: `NoTap`, the quota edge, the window an hour early and on time, and a cancel that keeps the quota used;
  - `tapYoungestFirst`: lots whose slot order, reverse slot order and age order all differ;
  - `tapSealForfeit`: a Tap pauses a Seal and its execution forfeits it;
  - `tapStreakResetBetween`: an ordinary execution between a Tap's request and its execution;
  - `tapAfterDeed`: a sale between Taps, with the buyer's first Tap at 90 days of the new Streak;
  - `tapLotCapacity`: the split that cannot reserve a slot, and the whole-lot Tap that can;
  - `taps0`–`taps2`: random walks with Taps, cooling, cancels, executions, Seals and deposits, each of which must Tap at least three times.
- **Unit tests:**
  - every tier edge, to the second;
  - the tier following the Streak, not the position's age;
  - the quota exactly met and exceeded by one wei, and taken of active principal only;
  - lots tied at the age cap drawn from the lower slot;
  - the ticket flag and the event;
  - no haircut and no Streak reset, with the fee paid;
  - the listing lock;
  - gas.
- **ForgeView** decodes `lastTapAt` and the ticket's `tap` (a dedicated test).
- **ForgePool invariants:**
  - the handler now Taps;
  - a new invariant shows that executing a Tap leaves the Streak and every surviving lot's age as they were;
  - the existing ones hold across Taps: custody backed, tokens conserved, emergency exit always possible, the Seal paused exactly while a ticket is pending.
- **Mutations:** 30 planted bugs on these paths. **All 30 are caught.** They cover:
  - the execution: a haircut or a Streak reset on a Tap, a Tap that keeps its Seal, an ordinary execution without its haircut;
  - the request: pro-rata selection for Taps, youngest-first for cooling, the flag not stored, the event without it, the plan or the quota skipped, a cancel that refunds the quota;
  - the order: oldest first, slot order, ties to the higher slot;
  - the quota: each tier edge off by a second, one tier's window or share used for another, the tier read from the position's age, no gate, no window, a window a second short, no quota, the quota exclusive, the quota of custody, `lastTapAt` not set;
  - ForgeView: both new decoding offsets.
- **Three gaps found and closed.** Three mutants were caught only by a random walk, because a scripted replay checks that a call reverts, not why:
  - tiers read from the position's age;
  - the quota taken of custody;
  - ties going to the higher slot.
  Each now has a unit test, and each test was checked to fail against its mutant.

## Consequences

- **Long-Streak owners can take part of their stake without losing the Streak or the Heat of what stays.** Youngest-first raises the blended Heat of the remainder.
- **A Tap forfeits a running Seal.** The interface should say so before the request, as the config's Seal copy does.
- **Two hooks remain:** source-lot additions (with Casting's consumer, ADR 0013) and Kindling positions with the First Flame setter.
- **Not audited or deployed.**
