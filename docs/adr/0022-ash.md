# ADR 0022: ASH, its issuance ledger and the Kindling bonus

Status: accepted for this repository. Not audited, not deployed.

## Context

**What the spec requires** (v5.5 §1, §2, §3; `CONTRACTS.md` "Ignition" and "Hearth"; DECISIONS line 7; the config's `shareToken`):
- **The budget.** ASH has "70,000 cumulative economic issuance". Its allocations are "exactly 41,000 Ignition + 1,000 Kindling + 28,000 Forge". The config's validator requires the leaves to sum to 70,000, and the type note says "nothing else in the protocol can mint ASH".
- **The Forge's share** "vests over 730 days using cumulative integer entitlement, never a rounded daily payment authority": `V(t) = floor(allocation · min(elapsed, duration) / duration)`, with a payout between checkpoints of `V(t1) − V(t0)`.
- **Burns.** "Neither a burn nor an unclaimed balance creates an additional ASH allocation." "A bridged ASH credit is transport, not another allocation."
- **The token** is OFT-compatible, with transport initially inactive. It has 18 decimals on EVM and no transfer tax.
- **The Kindling bonus.** Kindling retains "the … 1,000-ASH bonus leaf".

**What exists.**
- The registry already holds a mined `AshToken` address.
- Kindling (ADR 0021) records each wallet's net principal and exposes `status`, `totalNet` and `contributions` as views.
- Ignition, the Forge's ASH stream and the Hearth (ASH staking) are not built.

## Decision

**1. Three immutable contracts in `contracts/src/ash/`.** None has an admin, a pause or an upgrade.

**2. `AshToken`.**
- **The token itself:** ERC-20, burnable, permit, with no transfer tax. Its name, symbol and budget come from the generated parameters.
- **Its only minter is `LEDGER`**, a constructor argument, so the ledger can deploy at its predicted address.
- **The cumulative budget is enforced in the token.** `totalMinted` counts every unit ever minted, burns included, and a mint that would pass 70,000 reverts (`IssuanceExceeded`). So a burn never makes room for a re-issue, whatever the live supply.

**3. `AshLedger`, ASH's issuance authority.** Its immutables are `TOKEN`, `IGNITION`, `KINDLING_BONUS`, `FORGE` and `START`.
- **`releaseIgnition()`:** permissionless, once. It mints 41,000 to `IGNITION`.
- **`releaseKindlingBonus()`:** `KINDLING_BONUS` only, once. It mints 1,000 to the bonus contract.
- **`releaseForge()`:** permissionless. It mints `V(now) − released` to `FORGE`. `vestedAt(t)` is the canonical `vested()` exactly, checked against generated vectors. Nothing is ever anticipated.
- **Together** these are the whole 70,000. There is no other mint path.

**4. `KindlingAshBonus`.** It pays the 1,000 pro rata to Kindling contributors after settlement.
- **Each wallet's share** is `floor(1,000 · net / totalNet)`, the same basis as its LP. It reads only Kindling's public views, so Kindling is unchanged.
- **Funding.** The first claim asks the ledger for the 1,000 and checks it arrived exactly (`FundingMismatch`).
- **Claims** are permissionless, per wallet (`claim`) or in pages (`claimMany`, which skips what is not owed). ASH only ever goes to the wallet.
- **The remainder.** The claim that places the last net principal burns the rounding remainder.

**5. Provisional readings [P].** Each was taken as recommended (the owner's standing rule) and is a deploy blocker until confirmed.
- **[P] A failed Kindling never mints the bonus.** The bonus refuses to claim before settlement, so the ledger's 1,000 stays unminted for good, as the EMBER seed stays reserved (ADR 0003).
- **[P] The bonus basis** is Kindling net principal at settlement, the LP's own basis. Gross would reward fees.
- **[P] The bonus's rounding remainder is burned.** ASH is cumulative, so nothing re-issues it.
- **[P] The Forge's 730 days start at the Mantle's `START`**, epoch 0.
- **[P] Ignition's 41,000 is released in one piece** to the Ignition contract, which owns its distribution. Ignition is ADR 0023.
- **[P] The cap lives in the token**, cumulative, as well as in the ledger's fixed amounts. This is defence in depth: no future minter could pass 70,000 either.
- **Not built here:**
  - transport (OFT);
  - the Forge's ASH distributor (`ForgeAsh`), which will stream the 28,000 by the pools' ASH weights 500/200/300/0;
  - Hearth staking;
  - Ignition.

**6. The kit.**
- `AshToken`, `AshLedger` and, with the real Kindling, `KindlingAshBonus` deploy at their CREATE3 addresses.
- The operator stands in for Ignition and for the Forge's ASH distributor. Without the real Kindling it also takes the bonus slot.
- The smoke run releases Ignition's test ASH to the operator.

## Size and gas

- **Size:** AshToken is 4,375 runtime bytes, AshLedger 2,000 and KindlingAshBonus 3,498.
- **Gas:**
  - `releaseIgnition`: 93,811;
  - `releaseForge`: 50,878;
  - the first bonus claim, which funds the bonus: 153,584;
  - a later bonus claim: 57,552.

## Verification

- **Vectors.** `pnpm gen` emits `ashVesting`: canonical `vested(28,000, t, 730 days)` at fixed points (edges, round days and odd seconds). `AshLedger.vestedAt` equals every one.
- **Unit and fuzz tests** (`Ash.t.sol`, 12):
  - **the token:** only the ledger mints; the budget counts burns;
  - **the Forge stream:** daily releases for 731 days sum to exactly 28,000, with `released = V(now)` after each; releases at fuzzed times equal the curve;
  - **the other allocations:** each is released once to its recipient, the 70,000 is reached exactly and never passed, and zero inputs are refused;
  - **the bonus on a real Kindling:**
    - pro rata on net, paid to the wallet and not the caller, with only the remainder burned;
    - pages skip what is not owed;
    - nothing before settlement, and never minted after a failure;
    - a short funding refused;
    - every claim order fuzzed to pay the whole bonus.
- **Kit.**
  - The default mode checks the wiring and the operator slots.
  - The real-Kindling mode claims the bonus after settlement. The failed event refuses it and never mints it.
  - LiteForge fork dry run: 55 transactions and 91.9M gas by default; 33 and 94.0M with the real Kindling.
- **Mutations:** see the PR.

## Consequences

- **ASH exists**, with its whole budget fixed and enforced twice. Ignition (ADR 0023) can be built on `releaseIgnition`.
- **Mainnet gate:** `AshLedger` and `KindlingAshBonus` are built slots. `ForgeAsh` is a new unbuilt slot, for 31 blockers.
