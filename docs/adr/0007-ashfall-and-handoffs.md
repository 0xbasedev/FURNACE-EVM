# ADR 0007: Ashfall distribution and the Seal-ledger consumers

**Status:** Accepted; provisional where marked [P].
**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Config:** v5.5.0 · **Code:** `HeatMath.sol` (Heat²), `ForgePool.sol` and `ForgeMeasures.sol` (Streak anchors, Ashfall weight), `ForgeEmissions.sol` (Ashfall pass, consumers), `Mantle.sol` (`burnForfeited`) · **Reference:** `scripts/forge-model.ts`, `scripts/settlement-model.ts`

## Context

ADR 0005 and ADR 0006 listed what `ForgeEmissions` had to gain before any deployment:
- Ashfall distribution;
- the Capstone handoff;
- the consumers of the burn-lane, match-reserve and Sealed-pot ledgers.

The Mantle's `DISTRIBUTOR` is immutable and only it can call `mintAshfall` and `rollAshfall`, so none of this can be added later. ForgePool is immutable too, and Ashfall needs history it did not keep.

The spec (§8, §12) and config (`emissions.ashfall`, `execution.ashfall`) define Ashfall:
- **Funding:** burns recognized by the Mantle, at their source rates (Protocol 50%, Pyre 25%), with a one-epoch lag. Cap-constrained credits queue, and an epoch nobody was eligible in carries forward.
- **Eligibility:** a lot needs 30 days of actual participation, and its position needs an active Streak of at least the `minRelic` Relic (Flame: 30 days). Founder phantom age does not count.
- **Weight:** stake × ordinary Heat², with no Seal coefficient, from the canonical `squaredHeatPrimitive`.
- **Emergency exit** forfeits the position's pending Ashfall to everyone who stayed.

## Decisions

**1. Heat² is an exact port.**
- `HeatMath.squaredHeatPrimitive` and `integrateSquaredHeat` equal canonical `accounting.ts` bit for bit on generated vectors: every edge of every milestone, the cap, past the cap, and random ordinary bonuses up to 0.65.
- **Radicand above 256 bits.** The reference's `T5(x) = isqrt(x⁵·RAY²/R)` needs about 280 bits. The port runs Newton's integer square root, taking `⌊N/s⌋ = ⌊x⁵·RAY²/(R·s)⌋` from `mulDiv`. It starts from a proven over-estimate, `1e9·(isqrt(⌊x⁵·1e36/R⌋) + 1)`, so it converges in a few steps to the exact floor.
- **Constants.** The roots at the five milestones are generated by `pnpm gen` from the canonical BigInt code. Only the live endpoints need computing.
- **Signed term.** The reference's `(3·d5 − 5·left·d3)` is proportional to ∫(x−left)√x dx ≥ 0, and its floor errors (at most 3 + 5·left) are far smaller than its value for any interval of a second or more. A checked subtraction therefore never underflows, and BigInt truncation equals the floor.
- **A difference skips its common intervals.** `P(to) − P(from)` sums the same floored terms for every interval ending at or before `from`, so those cancel exactly and are skipped. This keeps the port exact for any ordinary bonus (Capstone or Pyre later) and costs about 21k gas per segment, at any age.

**2. Records carry the Streak anchor.**
- `Record` gains `streak`, the position's Streak start while that head was current. It stays one storage slot (248 bits). The head's own anchor is the position's current `streakStart`.
- **Why it is exact.** A Streak resets only on an executed withdrawal, and `_haircut` rewrites every active head at that instant, before `streakStart` moves. So an active record segment never spans a reset, and its anchor is constant over it.
- **Invariant.** `invariant_streakAnchorsAlignWithRecords` checks that every active segment's anchor is at or before its start, and that the next segment's anchor is either the same or a reset at the boundary.
- **Any future Streak reset** (a Deed sale, a Tap rule) must rewrite the active heads first, or this guarantee breaks.

**3. The Ashfall weight.**
- Per record segment `[a, b]`, eligibility starts at `e = max(a, anchor + 30d, a + (30d − participation at a))`. Participation grows 1:1 while the lot is active, so one start time suffices.
- The weight is `stake · (P(age at b) − P(age at e))`, with ordinary Heat only.
- **[P] Rounding.** The canonical package defines no Ashfall measure scale. The weight is kept exactly as a quotient and a remainder modulo RAY, and floored by RAY once per position-epoch: `Q_i = ⌊Σ stake·∫Heat² / RAY⌋`. It therefore does not depend on how records subdivide the epoch (the same rule as C, ADR 0006).
- **Model check.** The custody model accrues the same weight independently of the records, from its own lot participation and Streak, and the differential compares them.

**4. Ashfall in ForgeEmissions.**
- **[P] Pool split.** `openEpoch` splits the Mantle's epoch Ashfall by the base weights 1000/400/400/200. The spec defines no Ashfall pool split, and §4 forbids one denominator across pools. The split's floor residue, and the share of any pool with nothing admitted, roll back at once.
- **Heat² only when needed.** A pool-epoch measures Ashfall only when its share is nonzero, so until burn registrars exist settlement pays no Heat² gas.
- **[P] Page budget.** While Ashfall is measured, each record segment costs `ASHFALL_SEGMENT_UNITS` = 2 of the 256-unit page budget.
- **Crediting.** Credit pages add `⌊share·Q_i/Q⌋` to the position's claimable rewards, minted with one `mintAshfall` per page, and claims cover base and Ashfall alike.
- **What rolls back to the Mantle's queue,** where the next finalized epoch re-reserves it:
  - a pool's share when nobody was eligible (`Q = 0`, config `rolloverUndistributed`);
  - the floor residue;
  - the shares of forfeited positions.
- **[P] "Pending" Ashfall is what has not been credited yet.** A position that left by emergency exit forfeits any Ashfall not credited when the credit page reaches it. Ashfall credited before the exit stays its own, like base rewards. The outcome therefore depends on when the keeper runs relative to the exit.

**5. Every held ledger has one consumer.**
- **[P] Burn lane.** Permissionless `burnForfeits()` burns it through the Mantle's distributor-only `burnForfeited`. That is a recognized burn (the ledger shrinks, headroom reopens) with **no Ashfall credit**, because Seal forfeits are not among the config's Ashfall sources.
- **Pulls.** The Capstone (`takeCapstone(pool, id)`), the match reserve (`takeMatchReserve()`) and the Sealed-pot stream (`takeSealedPot(pool)`) pull their ledgers.
  - Their addresses are immutable and must be nonzero at deployment.
  - Until a consumer exists, its EMBER stays in `ForgeEmissions`.
  - No consumer address is invented: these identities are not in the registry and still have to be mined.
- **[P] Sealed-pot stream period.** The config does not define it, and this repo does not choose one. The stream contract decides it.

## Provisional defaults

| Topic | Default here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ashfall across pools | Base weights 1000/400/400/200 | The spec defines no Ashfall split; one cross-pool denominator is forbidden (§4) |
| Ashfall weight rounding | Exact within a position-epoch, floored once by RAY | Independent of record subdivision |
| Heat² page cost | 2 units per segment | Heat² roughly doubles a segment's cost |
| "Pending" Ashfall at emergency exit | Not yet credited | Credited rewards are the holder's, as with base |
| Seal-forfeit burns | Recognized, 0% Ashfall credit | Forfeits are not a configured Ashfall source |
| Ledger handoffs | Pulled by immutable consumers | The consumers can be built later at fixed addresses |
| Sealed-pot stream period | Not chosen here | Absent from the config |

## Still required before any deployment

These were found or confirmed here and are **not built**:
- **The Capstone Heat bonus.** It is ordinary Heat (up to +0.5) and must enter W and the Ashfall weight. ForgePool's measures take no ordinary bonus today. ForgePool is immutable and about 0.7 KB under the size limit, so the hook needs a structural change before deployment.
- **The Capstone, the matching and match-reserve contracts, and the Sealed-pot stream contract,** at mined addresses.
- **The emergency exit's "stayers" share** (25% of the 8% fee) still goes to `FEE_COLLECTOR` with the rest of the fee.
- **Relic perks** (shorter claim timers), **Quote yield** and **Pyre**.

## Gas (`pnpm gas`: forge `--isolate`, optimizer 500 runs, solc-js 0.8.28)

Every call is its own transaction, so storage starts cold. Each Ashfall case measures its costliest ages: below the cap, where every segment needs live Heat² roots.

| Path | Gas | Without Ashfall |
|---|---:|---:|
| Measure page: 32 positions with 1 lot each (ages ~32 days) | 5,480,677 | 3,925,366 |
| Measure page: 1 position with 32 lots | 1,766,863 | 831,843 |
| Credit page: 32 earners, base + Ashfall (two mints) | 1,319,016 | 1,369,840 (one mint) |
| Record-spammed epoch (1,200 records at the cap): heaviest page | 3,375,529, in 10 pages | 5,360,350, in 5 pages |
| `integrateSquaredHeat`, one segment (in-test, any age) | ≈21,000 | — |

- **The Ashfall credit page is not more expensive.** It reads cheaper only because its earners already had nonzero `unclaimed` slots from an earlier epoch. Each Ashfall earner also reads its Measure's extra slot and, if it has a weight, calls `positionOf` once.
- **Worst case.** At 2 units per segment, a page examines at most 128 segments and 32 slots.
  - **Cheap record spam needs lots at the age cap,** because only there does a 1-wei slice merge back into its own slot, and at the cap Heat² is cheapest.
  - **Below the cap,** a returning slice's frozen age differs from its lot's, so each request-and-cancel round takes a new slot, and the 32-lot cap ends the rounds.
  - **Estimate, not measured:** a page of 128 sub-cap segments at roughly 35k each (Heat, Heat² and a cold record) plus slot overhead stays near the 5.5M measured above.
- **Without Ashfall** (every epoch until burns are registered), a page pays no Heat² cost.

## Verification

- **Heat² vectors:** 94 primitive values (47 ages, with and without an ordinary bonus) and 32 integrals, all exactly equal to canonical BigInt.
- **Custody differential (22 scenarios):** after every action, the contract's record-based Ashfall weight must equal the model's independent accrual. 16 scenarios reach a nonzero weight, including random walks with Streak resets. Generation fails if fewer than 12 do.
  - `ashfallStreakReset` aims at the anchor itself. An eligible position resets its Streak, then pushes a record within the next 30 days. The records pushed at the reset must keep the old anchor, and the later one the new anchor.
- **Settlement differential (18 scenarios):** the new `ashfallHeatSquared` scenario covers:
  - a registered burn funding the next epoch;
  - the pool split, with an empty pool and an ineligible pool rolling their shares;
  - a Streak reset making eligibility start mid-epoch;
  - an emergency exit forfeiting an uncredited share;
  - the rolled queue paid to stayers in the next epoch.

  `sealForfeitRouted` and `sealVestLate` now end with the burn and the three pulls. The full state (Mantle Ashfall budgets, queue, credits and ledger; every pool's share, paid and Q; progress and measures; consumer balances) must match after every action.
- **Invariants:**
  - **Custody:** the Streak anchors align with the records.
  - **Settlement** (`fail_on_revert`), with random burns, long quiet stretches and consumer pulls, starting from 31 settled days so Ashfall is live. Probe runs credited about 130,000 to 150,000 EMBER of Ashfall each:
    - per epoch, Ashfall minted + rolled ≤ budget, and = budget once every pool is done;
    - a pool pays at most its share;
    - minted Ashfall equals what pools credited;
    - EMBER held equals every ledger;
    - EMBER supply equals the Mantle's `accounted`.
- **Mutations:** 17 planted bugs, each run in its own worktree against every suite. **All 17 are caught.**
  - **Heat²:** dropping the root term, skipping Newton's refinement, and not subtracting the partial term at `from` are each caught by the exact vectors.
  - **Streak anchors:** a record that stores no anchor, resetting the Streak before the haircut, and measuring every record with the head's anchor are each caught by the custody differential (`ashfallStreakReset` and others).
  - **Eligibility and rounding:** ignoring the participation minimum, ignoring the Streak minimum, and flooring the weight per segment instead of once are each caught by the custody differential.
  - **Ashfall pass:** paying a share to an empty pool, keeping the share when nobody is eligible, crediting an emergency-exited position, not rolling the floor residue, and not charging Heat² page units are each caught (the last also by the page-gas bounds).
  - **Consumers:** crediting Ashfall for forfeit burns, an unguarded `takeCapstone`, and `burnForfeits` keeping its ledger are each caught.

## Consequences and limits

- **Size:** ForgePool is 23,870 bytes (706 below EIP-170), ForgeEmissions 16,571 bytes.
- **Cost:** an epoch with Ashfall settles in about twice as many measure pages for record-heavy positions.
- **Not audited or deployed.** Matching the models proves consistency with this ADR, not canonical conformance of any [P] rule.
