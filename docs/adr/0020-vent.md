# ADR 0020: The Vent, the fee router

Status: accepted for this repository. Not audited, not deployed.

## Context

**What the spec requires** (v5.5 §13; `CONTRACTS.md` "FeeRouter (Vent)"; the config's `fees.vent`, `fees.burnLane`, `fees.feeVault`, `fees.liquidityLane`, `execution.vent`, `forge.emergencyExit.routing` and `forge.deeds.feeRouting`):
- **The splits.**
  - "Standard Forge fees follow 40/40/10/10": burn, liquidity, Hearth, Treasury.
  - Emergency-exit fees follow their own schedule, 50 burn / 25 liquidity / 25 stakers.
  - Deed fees, 1% of a sale, follow 50/50.
  - Capstone fees burn 100%; Boost already burns them (ADR 0009).
- **Timing.** "Book liabilities at receipt, process safe conversions later. A failed burn conversion must not prevent principal exit or make Treasury the beneficiary of staker liabilities."
- **The burn lane** unwinds and buys back:
  - "the $5,000 batch threshold or 24h eligibility";
  - an 1800s TWAP, a 2% average-price band, a 1% slippage cap and a 0.5% per-call reserve chunk;
  - "a cumulative 0.5% quote-reserve limit per 1800-second window measured against its fixed starting reserve. Repeated `vent()` calls cannot reset that window or enlarge the base with a donation";
  - "a failed check fails closed: the quote asset waits in the fee vault for the next vent() call";
  - "The 72-hour deferral is an alert/escalation threshold, never an override."
- **The liquidity lane.** "Fee LP is unwound, the EMBER half burned, the quote half held to pair with Stokes." "Reserve quote is not Treasury-spendable."
- **Burns** are "unique authenticated economic burns" that "actually destroy economic token supply and create one typed burn receipt". A transfer to a dead address is not one.
- **The module itself.** It has "no issuance authority; no public arbitrary recipient/route". `neverAllowed` includes `redirectVent`, and "permissionless means anyone may submit a valid transaction".

**What exists.**
- Every pool's `collectFees()` pays `FEE_COLLECTOR` its deposit, withdrawal and emergency fees in the stake token (ADR 0004).
- Every pair's `collectProtocolFees()` pays `FEE_ROUTER` its burn, liquidity and Quote-yield fees in quote, split by lane (ADR 0015).
- The pairs keep a 30-minute TWAP (`consult`).
- The Mantle's `burnRegistered` burns a registrar's EMBER and books its Ashfall credit (ADR 0003).
- On the testnet, the operator's wallet held both fee slots.

## Decision

**1. One immutable contract, `contracts/src/vent/Vent.sol`.** It has no admin, pause or upgrade.
- **Its sources:** `EMBER`, `MANTLE`, the four pools and the two pairs.
- **Its fixed recipients:** `HEARTH`, `TREASURY`, `STAKERS` and `QUOTE_YIELD`.
- **Its two gated callers:**
  - `DEEDS`, the only booker of Deed fees;
  - `COMPOUNDER`, the only drawer of match-reserve quote, the same slot Flow and the LP pools name.
  - A zero address disables either.
- **What the constructor calls.** Nothing on the pools or pairs, since the pairs are an add-on (ADR 0015). It calls only the Mantle, and refuses to exist unless the Mantle registered it as a Protocol burner (`NotRegistrar`), because its burns would otherwise earn nothing.
- **Accounting is in ledgers, never in balances**, so a donation changes nothing.

**2. Collection** is permissionless and pulls only from the immutable sources.
- `collectPool(i)` books deposit and withdrawal fees at the standard split, and emergency fees at the emergency split.
- `collectPair(j)` books the pair's three lanes as they come.
- `collectAll()` tries each source and skips one that reverts. Before the pairs exist, pool 2's EMBER fees still burn, while pool 3's and the LP pools' fees wait in the pools.
- `bookDeedFee(token, amount)` is `DEEDS`-only, with an exact pull (`NonStandardToken`). A public booking would let a donation earn Protocol Ashfall credit, and the config credits unsolicited burns at 0%.

**3. Routing by asset**, one rule for every source. Every share is floored and the burn lane takes the remainder.
- **A pair's LP is redeemed at once.** Redemption is pro-rata, needs no oracle and is never paused. A manipulated pool only raises its value at the true price.
  - One redeem covers both kinds. Each side is split by the kinds' LP weights, and the rounding remainder joins the emergency kind.
  - LP worth less than a wei on both sides stays unbooked.
- **EMBER:** the burn and liquidity shares burn now, through `MANTLE.burnRegistered`. That is the liquidity lane's "EMBER half burned".
- **A pair's quote** goes into that pair's burn lane (`burnQuote`) or match reserve (`matchReserve`). Pool 3's USDC is BlastPool's quote.
- **The Hearth, Treasury, stakers' and Quote-yield shares are owed in kind.** `release(recipient, token)` pushes them, one recipient at a time, so one that refuses blocks no other.

**4. `vent(pair)`, the buyback.** Anyone may call it. It never reverts on an unsafe market: it returns a `Deferral` and emits `VentDeferred`, and the quote waits. The checks run in order:
1. **Eligibility:** the lane is draining, or 24 hours have passed since it became nonempty, or it holds 5,000 USDC (BlastPool only).
2. **The pair:** swaps live (`Paused`), liquidity and a `consult(1800)` that answers (`NoOracle`), a TWAP span of at most an hour (`Stale`), and spot within 2% of the TWAP (`Band`).
3. **The window:** the window's base is the quote reserve at its first in-band call. The spend is `min(burn lane, 0.5% of the current reserve, the window's remaining 0.5% of its base)` (`Window`).
4. **The floor:** the minimum out is the spend's TWAP value less 1%, the pair's 25 bps included (`Unsafe`).
5. **Then** `pair.buy` and `burnRegistered`. The Vent pays the public fee; its own 5 bps protocol part returns as fees.
6. **The alert:** a deferral after 72 hours of waiting also emits `VentOverdue`. Nothing about the guards changes.

**5. `drawMatchReserve(pair, amount)`** is `COMPOUNDER`-only, up to the reserve (`ExceedsReserve`).
- The consumer keeps its reservations (spec §7), so quote left in the Vent is unreserved.
- The Treasury cannot reach it.

**6. Provisional readings [P].** Each was taken as recommended and is a deploy blocker until the owner confirms it.
- **[P] Fee assets.** LP fees are unwound and routed in kind, the Hearth's and Treasury's shares included. The liquidity lane burns EMBER and holds quote, for every asset. Pool 3's USDC buys back in BlastPool. The spec defines the unwind for LP only.
- **[P] The emergency stakers' 25%** is owed to an immutable `STAKERS` distributor, which is not built. ADR 0007 had left it with the collector.
- **[P] The batch threshold** applies only to BlastPool, whose quote is a USD stablecoin: 5,000 whole units. SmelterPool uses the 24-hour clock alone. No USD oracle is invented.
  - Once a buyback starts, the lane drains until empty (`draining`).
- **[P] Windows are aligned** (`block.timestamp / 1800`), so two windows can meet at a boundary. Each is still capped against its own fixed base.
- **[P] The oracle is stale** when `consult`'s span exceeds twice the window. Keepers checkpoint the pairs; the Vent never does.
- **[P] The 3% price-impact cap is implied.** A buy of at most a 0.5% chunk moves the price about 1%. The generator refuses any config where the chunk and the floor would not imply the cap, and a fuzz test asserts it on every executed buyback.
- **[P] Every Vent burn is a Protocol burn**: Forge, swap, emergency and Deed fees alike, as the Mantle's own category comment reads. The config lists Deed fees in no Ashfall source.
- **[P] The Vent is core and immutable**, per §13's "immutable" wording and `redirectVent`.

## Verification

- **Model.**
  - `scripts/pair-model.ts` gains the oracle: the cumulative price, the 8-entry ring written every 300 s, `checkpoint` and `consult`, plus the pause and the buy preview. The pair and Flow vectors are unchanged by it.
  - `scripts/vent-model.ts` models the ledgers, the routing by kind and asset, the LP unwind, eligibility, the windows and every guard on top of it.
  - `pnpm gen` writes `contracts/test/fixtures/vent-vectors.json`.
- **Differential.** `Vent.t.sol` replays the model's 10 scenarios on two real EmberPairs, four mock fee pools and a mock Mantle. It compares every output and every revert, plus a hash of the whole state after every step: the lanes and windows, the owed and received amounts, the Vent's balances, the pairs' reserves, fees and TWAP, the pools' ledgers and the burns.
  - The scripted scenarios cover routing, dust, each guard in turn, the window, the clock and Deed fees with draws.
  - Four seeded random walks mix every action with time.
  - Across them: 35 buybacks, every deferral reason, and 30 LP unwinds.
- **Unit tests:**
  - the constructor and the registrar check;
  - splits by kind and asset;
  - the LP unwind and dust;
  - `collectAll` skipping a failing source;
  - every gate on Deed fees and draws, a short-delivering token included;
  - a recipient that refuses;
  - a buyback, then the window and the floor deferring;
  - donations;
  - the batch and the clock;
  - the alert;
  - a fuzz test of the impact cap.
- **Invariants** (`VentInvariant.t.sol`, over random trades, fees, collections, buybacks, releases, draws, Deed fees, donations, pauses and time):
  - each token the Vent holds is exactly its ledgers plus what was donated;
  - no window spends more than 0.5% of its base;
  - every burn went through the Mantle, credit and all;
  - every buyback kept its chunk, window and impact cap, and every deferral spent nothing;
  - match-reserve quote reached only the compounder, and owed shares only their recipients, exactly what each release paid.
  - In a probe of one invariant's 129 runs, 66 executed buybacks.
- **Kit.**
  - The Vent deploys at its predicted CREATE3 address. It is registered with the Mantle as a Protocol burner, and it is every pool's `FEE_COLLECTOR` and every pair's `FEE_ROUTER`. The operator stands in for the recipients, `DEEDS` and `COMPOUNDER`, and the wiring check enforces all of it.
  - The smoke run collects and calls `vent`, which defers on a fresh pair.
  - A kit test then lets the TWAP mature and the clock run. It shows a real buyback, the EMBER supply falling by what was bought, the Mantle's credit, a release, and an emergency exit unaffected.
  - The mainnet gate's `FeeRouter` note reads built, and it gains `Hearth`, `Stakers` and `QuoteYield` slots, for 26 blockers.
- **Rehearsal.** `pnpm testnet:dry-run` on a fork of LiteForge, with the smoke actions, ran 52 transactions: 89.4M gas, about 0.0018 zkLTC at 0.02 gwei. The largest is a ForgePool deployment at 12.48M.
- **Mutations:** see the PR.

## Consequences

- **The pools' and pairs' fees have a real router**, and nothing an administrator can redirect.
- **Still unbuilt:** the Hearth, the stakers' and Quote-yield distributors, the Deed market, and the Stoke-into-LP and Casting consumer that draws the match reserve.
- **Next (step 12):** the permanent LP sink and idle auto-pairing (7 idle epochs, funded Treasury match-seed EMBER), with Kindling, whose 10% LP sink is the same contract.
- **Not audited or deployed.**
