# ADR 0016: Flow, the swap and zap entrypoint

Status: accepted for this repository. Not audited, not deployed.

## Context

**What the spec requires** (v5.5 §4, §11 and §14; `CONTRACTS.md`; the config's `swap.zap`, `fees.swap.waiveProtocolShareOnCompound`, `forge.fees.waiveDepositFeeFromFlow` and `relics[].perks.feeRebatePct`):
- **Flow's job.** "Authenticated swaps and optional owner-authorized zaps. Waivers are action capabilities, never user-provided flags" (`CONTRACTS.md`). "Keep protocol CPMM routing, LP rows, explicit zap/unzap paths" (§14).
- **Relic rebates.** Eternal rebates 25% of the protocol's swap-fee share and Bedrock 50%. A rebate is "funded pro-rata from the burn/liquidity/quote-yield slices", and "current perks require active state rather than possession of an old NFT" (§9).
- **The Compound waiver.** `waiveProtocolShareOnCompound`: a Compound pays no protocol share. "Off-protocol routers cannot obtain special Compound privileges by pretending to be Forge" (§14).
- **The deposit-fee exemption.** "Authenticated same-route Flow LP formation retains its specified deposit-fee exemption. A public boolean cannot manufacture that exemption. Source exclusion for a waived fee means no burn credit is created for a fee not charged" (§4).
- **Selling needs consent.** "A selling zap is a separately authorized route with explicit owner consent, min-output, deadline, and the expected exposure shown" (§4).
- **Zap defaults.** 50 bps default slippage, a 300 bps maximum, and `stakeAfterZap: 'offer'`.

**What already exists.**
- **The pairs' side** (ADR 0015). `flowBuy` and `flowSell` accept only the immutable `FLOW` and take the protocol-share scale. The LP fee is never reduced.
- **The pools' side.** It does not exist. A ForgePool always charges its 1% deposit fee, and it is immutable, so the exemption's hook must exist before any deployment.

## Decision

**1. Flow owns no funds and has no admin.**
- **Immutables:** EMBER, the two pairs, Forge pools 0 and 1 (which stake each pair's receipt), `REBATES` and `COMPOUNDER`.
- **No external call at construction**, so Flow, the pairs and the pools can deploy in any order at their CREATE3 addresses.
- **Every call:**
  - pulls exactly what it needs;
  - approves the pair or pool for exactly the amount to spend;
  - refunds or delivers everything before returning.
  Between calls Flow holds nothing.
- **It is guarded** by the transient-storage reentrancy guard.
- **It checks** a deadline and a minimum output on every route.

**2. Routes:**
- `swap(pair, buy, amountIn, minOut, to, deadline)`: an exact-in swap at the caller's rebate share.
- `zapIn(pair, fromQuote, amountIn, minLiquidity, deadline)`: single-sided liquidity. It swaps the computed part, then adds liquidity, then refunds dust; the receipts go to the caller.
- `zapAndStake(pair, fromQuote, amountIn, minLiquidity, positionId, deadline)`: the same zap, staked fee-free. `positionId` 0 opens a position for the caller; any other id deposits into the caller's own position. This is the spec's "same-route Flow LP formation": only receipts minted in this call can be staked this way.
- `zapOut(pair, liquidity, toQuote, minOut, deadline)`: it redeems the caller's receipts and sells the other side at the caller's share. This is the selling zap. The caller's own signature, the minimum output and the deadline are the consent. The interface must show the exposure first.
- `compoundSwap(pair, buy, amountIn, minOut, to, deadline)`: `COMPOUNDER` only, at a protocol share of 0. `COMPOUNDER` is the future Stoke-into-LP or Casting consumer, which ADR 0013 requires to be a single consumer fixed before deployment. A zero `COMPOUNDER` means no route has the waiver.

**3. The rebate.**
- `REBATES.rebatePctOf(wallet)` is read for `msg.sender` inside a try/catch. A revert, or malformed return data, counts as no rebate: a broken source costs the protocol nothing and never blocks a swap.
- **The value snaps down to a configured tier** (0, 25 or 50). That bounds what a faulty source can give away to the top tier.
- The share passed to the pair is `10⁶ − tier·10⁴`.
- The tiers are generated from `relics[].perks.feeRebatePct`.
- Relics, which decide a wallet's current tier, are not built. The testnet uses a stand-in the operator sets.

**4. The zap split** (the derivation is below).
- **The split.** For a single-sided input `a`, Flow swaps the `s` at which the remainder and the swap's output stand at the pool's post-swap ratio.
- **Fee model.** Each fee component is taken as proportional to its base (`φ` the total fee, `π` the protocol part, `λ` the LP part, at the caller's share). With that model, `s` solves a quadratic, which Flow evaluates in its cancellation-free form with integer ppm:
  - **from the quote (a buy):** `s = 2·a·rQ·10⁶ / (rQ·(2·10⁶−F) + √(rQ·(rQ·(2·10⁶−F)² + 4·(10⁶−Π)·(10⁶−F)·a)))`;
  - **from EMBER (a sell, whose fee comes from its output):** `s = 2·a·rE·10⁶ / (B + √(B² + 4·(10⁶−Π)·10⁶·a·rE))`, where `B = rE·(2·10⁶−F) − a·Λ`.
  - `F`, `Π` and `Λ` are those rates in ppm.
- **Rounding.** The pair's fees round up and the model is proportional, so the add leaves dust on one side. Flow refunds it.
- **Size limit.** Overflow is checked and reverts. Sizes far beyond any real reserve, around 2²⁰⁰ in the square, refuse to zap; such a holder can swap and add directly.

**5. The pool hook (ADR 0004).**
- **The argument.** ForgePool takes an immutable `FLOW`, and only an LP pool may set one: the exemption is for LP formation, so pools 2 and 3 pass zero.
- **The calls.**
  - `openFor(owner, amount)` and `depositFor(id, owner, amount)` accept only `FLOW` as caller and pull from it.
  - They charge no deposit fee and book none. Nothing is booked, so no burn credit can exist.
- **Every other rule holds:** `MIN_STAKE_LP`, the 32-lot capacity, a closed position, the Deed listing lock, and the bonus dilution on a deposit.
  - `depositFor` also requires `owner` to own the position. Flow passes `msg.sender`, so nobody can push lots into another person's position.
- **The annex** gets the same constructor argument, so the immutables stay equal (ADR 0012). Immutables take no storage, so `check:layout` is unchanged.

**6. What stays in the interface.**
- **Slippage.** The defaults (50 bps, a 300 bps cap) become each call's `minOut` or `minLiquidity`. On-chain, the quote at execution is the execution, so Flow cannot check slippage itself.
- **Staking after a zap.** `stakeAfterZap: 'offer'` means the interface offers `zapAndStake` and does not force it.

## The zap split, derived

**Buy side.** Swap `s` of quote. With proportional fees, the reserves after the swap are:
- `rE′ = rE·rQ / (rQ + (1−φ)s)`;
- `rQ′ = rQ + (1−π)s`;
- the swap's output is `e = rE·(1−φ)s / (rQ + (1−φ)s)`.

The condition `(a − s)·rE′ = rQ′·e` reduces to `(1−π)(1−φ)s² + rQ(2−φ)s − a·rQ = 0`.
- Its positive root is `s = 2a·rQ / (rQ(2−φ) + √(rQ²(2−φ)² + 4(1−π)(1−φ)a·rQ))`.
- With `π = 0` this is the textbook single-sided split.

**Sell side.** Swap `s` of EMBER. The gross output is `g = rQ·s/(rE+s)`, the trader receives `(1−φ)g`, and `rQ′ = rQ − (1−λ)g`.

The condition `(a − s)·rQ′ = (rE + s)·(1−φ)g` reduces to `(1−π)s² + (rE(2−φ) − aλ)s − a·rE = 0`, using `1 − φ + λ = 1 − π`. Its positive root is the form above.

- **Size.** Flow is 7,431 runtime bytes. The hooks take ForgePool from 20,973 to 21,569 bytes (3,007 under EIP-170) and its annex to 12,290.

## Verification

- **Model.** `scripts/flow-model.ts`, a BigInt model built on `pair-model.ts`, computes each split, the swap, the add, the refunds and the receipts. The generator writes `flow-vectors.json`, and the contract replays it exactly.
- **Unit tests:**
  - every route;
  - deadlines and minimums;
  - a rebate from each tier, from an off-tier value, and from a reverting source;
  - the waiver for `COMPOUNDER` only;
  - only Flow can call the pool's fee-free hooks, and only LP pools take a Flow;
  - `depositFor` into another person's position, and into a listed or closed one;
  - Flow holding nothing after any call.
- **Invariants** over random swaps, zaps, stakes and unzaps:
  - Flow's balances stay zero;
  - receipts staked equal receipts minted;
  - protocol fees booked equal the share rule's;
  - a zap leaves at most rounding dust.
- **Mutations:** 35 planted bugs, run in a separate worktree so the main tree never holds one. **All 35 are caught**, none needing a new test. They cover:
  - the rebate: no tier snapping, the first tier skipped, an uncapped tier, malformed data accepted, a reverting source propagating;
  - the waiver: open to anyone, open to a zero compounder, the share charged anyway;
  - deadlines and minimums: the deadline inclusive or dropped, the zap's and unzap's minimums ignored;
  - the split: the sides' rates swapped, the share ignored, the textbook (π = 0) form, the sell's LP term dropped, the root taken unscaled;
  - custody: either refund dropped, an allowance left behind, the stake sent to the wrong pool, each route ignoring the rebate;
  - the pool hook: either hook charging the fee, `open` fee-free, either hook ungated or open to a zero Flow, `depositFor` into another person's position, a Flow on a single-asset pool, the fee still booked;
  - the kit: Flow on every pool, the pairs not naming Flow.
- **Rehearsal.** `pnpm testnet:dry-run` with Flow and the smoke actions on a fork of LiteForge ran 43 transactions, the largest 10.44M gas. It caught one bug, which is fixed: the smoke zap's deadline was the script's own timestamp, already past when the broadcast replayed.

## Consequences

- **Rebates, the Compound waiver and fee-free zap-and-stake become reachable.** Until Relics and the compounder exist, the testnet's stand-ins drive them.
- **Every ForgePool deployment takes a sixth argument.** On mainnet, Flow is one more mined address that pools 0 and 1 and both pairs name.
- **Not audited or deployed.**
